# Formamorph v3.8.0

This update adds World Options. Worlds can offer choices, switches and sliders when you start a new game, so you can set up the story the way you want before it begins. Authors can tie traits, stats, items and even whole locations to them, and many can be changed mid-game.

I've also added Character Mentions. Once it's on, it works out who each "she" or "the courier" in the narration means, so every character's quotes show in their own color and hovering a name shows you who they are.

## Major

- 🧩 **World Options** — pick choices, switches and sliders at new game, or a Preset that sets them all in one click, to shape the story before it starts.
  - **Enter World** — clicking an entity or group opens one page with all of its traits, and the list folds to fit your screen.
- 💬 **Character Mentions** — each character's quotes show in their own color, and names and pronouns open a card with who they are.
  - **Wrong character?** — pick who a name or quote really means, and your save keeps the fix.

## New

- **Shared media** — the World Editor's Media tab lets you reuse a picture or sound anywhere in the world, so files that repeat are stored once.
- **Script images** — scripts can show pictures in the story, even right after the sentence they belong to.
- **Entity routines** — an entity can hold several routines and switch between them as the world changes, or be Away for a few hours.

## Fixes

Choices no longer give away the real name of someone you haven't met, long replies keep their last paragraph, scripts run faster on large worlds, and more.
