# Formamorph v3.9.0

This update adds weather. Every world has it now, from light rain to blizzards and sandstorms, and it plays over the location image while you read. The AI changes it when the story does, so a storm can roll in mid-scene and the narrator knows about it.

I've also added Layered Images, so a character's picture can be built from parts that change with the story. A torn shirt, a blush or a new outfit can show up on their image the moment it happens.

## Major

- ✨ **Weather** — every world has weather that moves over the location image and changes with the story, and the narrator hears about it every turn, so scenes feel like they happen somewhere real.
  - **Weather Masks** — paint where the weather shows on a location's image, so a Sheltered room can still see the rain through its window.
- 🖼️ **Layered Images** — build a character, location or item picture from stacked Layers that swap with traits, stats or scripts, so one image keeps up with the story.

## New

- **Requirement sentences** — the World Editor's Requires field reads each condition as a sentence you edit one word at a time, so rules are easier to read and write.
- **Opening names and times** — openings can have names and set the time of day a story starts, and traits can require the opening a game started from.
- **Character diaries** — read, edit and regenerate each character's diary in Memories, so you can steer what they remember.
- **Avatar edits in play** — change your Avatar's look mid-game from the pencil on your Avatar, and it stays after a rollback.

## Fixes

Characters who agree to come along follow you anywhere in their routine, more quotes take their speaker's color with Character Mentions on, large Supporter-only worlds download from Community Creations, and more.
