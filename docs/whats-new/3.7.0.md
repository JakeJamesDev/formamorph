# Formamorph v3.7.0

This update adds a few new features including a big rework of the entity placement system. Entities can now be set on a schedule with random movement or strict patrolling. This should make them feel like real people moving around the world.

I've also added a way for interacting with the UI to place a sort of 'what you did between turns' section so drinking a potion or handing someone an item is sent to the narration and acted out without you needing to step in.

## Major

- ⏳ **Since Last Turn** — a tray above the action box lists what you did between turns, lets you undo it, and tells the AI when you send.
- 🗓️ **Daily routines** — entities stay, wander or patrol by time of day, move about while you are elsewhere, and can follow you or wait until you come back with real restrictions on where they can go.
  - **Map strangers** — the in-game Map shows entities you haven't met, under a name you heard or as unknowns.

## New

- **Avatar looks from stats and traits** — stats and traits change Avatar sliders and colors with a live Preview in the World Editor.
- **Openings and Connections tabs** — openings get their own World Editor tab, and each Location's Connection can be Nearby or Far to properly separate them in context.

## Fixes

The item parser properly finds characters named by an alias, times of day like `evening` start on the hour, a failed turn puts your action back, and more.
