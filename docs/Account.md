# 👤 Your Account
<!-- keywords: sign in, sign up, signup, lost my password, new user, inbox, unread counter, join the site, credentials, data policy -->

A Community Creations account lets you publish, comment, follow and send Reports and feedback. This page covers logging in, your profile and deleting the account.

> Browsing and downloading in [Community Creations](Community-Creations) need no account.

## How to Change Your Profile Image
<!-- keywords: picture, photo, pfp, icon, account picture, upload, crop, display pic, user portrait, animated gif, account face, take off my pic, reposition, initial letter shown -->
<!-- route: profile -->

1. Log in.
2. On the main menu, select the round **User Profile** button at the bottom left.
3. Select your profile image.
4. Select a `.png`, `.jpg`, `.webp` or `.gif` file, up to 10 MB.
5. In **Position Your Profile Image**, drag the image to move it. Scroll, or use the **Zoom** slider, to zoom. The circle shows what everyone sees.
6. Select **Save**.

To remove the image, select **Remove your profile image**. Your initial then shows in its place. See [The User Profile Dialog](#the-user-profile-dialog).

## How to Delete Your Account
<!-- keywords: remove, close, erase, cancel, deactivate, my data, profile, gdpr, unregister, terminate membership, wipe my info, leave for good, right to erasure, undo deletion, privacy request -->
<!-- route: profile.settings -->

1. On the main menu, select the round **User Profile** button at the bottom left.
2. Select the **Settings** tab, then select **Delete Account**.
3. Read what happens, and select **Continue**.
4. Select **Delete My Work** or **Keep My Work**, and select **Continue**.
5. Type your password and select **Delete My Account**.

You are logged out immediately. The account is erased seven days later. To cancel, log in before that day. See [Account Deletion](#account-deletion).

## Login and Register
<!-- route: auth -->

Select the round button at the bottom left of the main menu to open **Login**. The [adult content warning](Community-Creations#the-adult-content-warning) comes first.

| Dialog | Fields |
|---|---|
| **Login** | **Username** and **Password**. **Forgot password?** opens the reset page on formamorph.ai. |
| **Register** | **Username**, **Password**, **Confirm Password** and **Email**, which is optional. The email lets you reset your password. |

The **Register** fields have these rules:

- **Username:** 3–20 letters, numbers, spaces, `_` or `-`. No two names can differ only by case, or by a space in place of `_`.
- **Password:** at least 6 characters.

Select **Create Account** to switch to **Register**, and **Back to Login** to switch back. To change your email, use the **Settings** tab of your **User Profile**, or the account page on formamorph.ai.

## The Privacy Policy
<!-- route: privacyPolicy -->

When you register, the privacy policy shows before the account is made. Select **Accept and Create Account**, or select **Decline** to make nothing.

When the policy changes, it opens after you log in:

| Choice | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Accept** | The prompt closes. |
| **Sign Out** | Logs you out. Your account doesn't change, and the policy asks again next time. |
| **Delete My Account** | Starts [account deletion](#account-deletion). |

Read the policy any time at [formamorph.ai/privacy](https://formamorph.ai/privacy).

## Choose a New Username
<!-- route: usernameRename -->

Some older usernames don't fit the username rule:

- They are too long or too short.
- They have symbols the rule doesn't allow.
- They match another account's name except for capital letters, or a space in place of `_`.

If yours is one of them, **Choose a New Username** opens when you log in or start the app.

- 💡 The **New Username** field starts with a suggested name when one is free. Keep it or type your own.
- Select **Save Username** to change it. From then on, you log in with the new name.
- Select **Later** to close the dialog. Your games and saves keep working.

When a rename is required, your account can't make changes until you rename. That includes likes, comments, follows and publishing. The dialog opens again when you try one.

Your listings, likes and comments move to the new name. Old links to your profile stop working.

## The User Profile Dialog
<!-- route: profile -->

Select the round button at the bottom left of the main menu. Its badge counts unread messages and new follow-feed items.

The dialog shows your profile image, **Member since** and your stats. Select your profile image to change it. See [How to Change Your Profile Image](#how-to-change-your-profile-image). The header has **Log Out**.

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Messages** | Messages from the Formamorph team, such as Report Outcomes. Dismiss a message with its button. |
| **Notifications** | The [follow feed](Community-Creations#the-follow-feed). |
| **Terms** | The [publishing terms](Community-Creations#publishing-terms). **Accept** or **Decline** them here. The tab shows only when terms exist. |
| **Settings** | Your **Email Address**, with **Save Email**. **Resend Verification Email** shows while the address is unverified. Your Patreon link, **Change Password** and **Delete Account** are here too. |

## Account Deletion
<!-- route: deleteAccount -->

The steps are in [How to Delete Your Account](#how-to-delete-your-account). The dialog has three steps:

| Step | What it asks |
|---|---|
| What happens | Nothing. It says the account is erased seven days from now, and nothing changes while you wait. |
| Your published work | **Delete My Work** removes your listings, their files and your comments. **Keep My Work** keeps them under the name *[deleted user]*. |
| Password | Your password, to confirm. |

The last screen names the date.

To cancel, log in before that date. The **Deletion Canceled** notice opens and says your account is unchanged. Select **OK**.

A suspended account can't be deleted from here. Select **Open Feedback** to ask the team.
