An AI text RPG that brings your world to life. Create your own characters and locations, or download any of the hundreds created by the community to explore.

                                      

Type what you do, or pick a suggested action. The narrator answers in full prose, and your stats change with the story. Every playthrough follows your choices.

  • A narrator that follows your lead. Stats, traits and locations update as the story moves.
  • Hundreds of worlds. Start with the worlds that come with the game, then download more from the community.
  • A full inventory. Equip gear, use a potion, drop what you don't need, or give it to someone in the scene.
  • A world editor. Write entities, locations, items, stats and scripts. Share your world as one file, or publish it to the community.
  • A 3D character of your own. Load any VRM model and shape its body, face and colors.
  • Your look. Pick a color theme, a font and how the story appears.
  • A built-in guide. Ask it questions as you play.

Connect any OpenAI-compatible AI service, or run a model on your own computer. The Connect Your Own AI guide shows how.
