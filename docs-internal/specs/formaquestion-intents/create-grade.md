# Create Probe: Hand Grade

The fit sample for the Create bar (Q62): **at least 80% of drafts fit the world and its tone**. Your grade decides the bar and the Creation default.

## How to grade

- Mark each row ✅ or ❌ in **Your grade**.
- A draft fits when it belongs in the world's setting and tone, takes a new name in the world's naming style, and is what the ask wanted. For a redraft, it makes the asked change and keeps the name.
- The **Judge** column is the cloud model grading itself. It misses names the world already uses, so the probe counts those apart (0 of 36 here).
- **Spot check** is the ticket session's own read.
