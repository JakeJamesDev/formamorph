D — Cell Simulation (Debug Reference)

What is really happening: the ungrouped board simulated through your whole gesture. Every cell your footprint travels over dodges behind you, backtracking reverses it, and a tile consents once enough of its cells have been swept. Ghost cells and sweep percentages show the machinery raw.

Player Preview

The same simulation, dressed for a player — no cells, no percentages. Cycle the treatments with the floating bar (or ← / →): Quiet Slide shows nothing until a tile truly moves. Pressure charges a bar across a swept tile as consent builds. Give Way leans the swept tile toward where it will go. The status line speaks player language. Grouping: park on a tile the drag cannot move and hold — after a beat it rings, and the release folds the carried tile into it. A tile that can make way always moves instead.
