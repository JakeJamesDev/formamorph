PROTOTYPEContent Reports — feel the flow

The question this answers: does the report lifecycle feel right? One-shot tickets (no threads) · one open report per person per thing · the staff queue groups reports by the reported thing and one resolution closes the whole group · every reporter gets an automatic inbox message (outcome + optional staff note, never the specific action) · content removed by its own author keeps its reports open · reports on staff-made content are visible to all staff but only resolvable by someone who outranks the author.
