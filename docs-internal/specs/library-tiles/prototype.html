PROTOTYPE — THROWAWAY
  1. Resize: right-click a tile → Small / Medium / Large. Consecutive small tiles stack in columns; four fill one medium slot. Drop on a small tile's top/bottom edge to stack.
  2. Group by drag: drag a tile onto the center of another → a folder forms. Edges = reorder instead.
  3. Enter a folder: click it. Rename by clicking the title. Reorder and resize inside. Back returns.
  4. Ungroup: inside a folder, drag a tile onto the header ("Drop here to move out"), or right-click → Remove from Group. Disband via right-click on the folder tile.
  5. Prompts (Worlds tab): right-click a folder → Group Prompt. Right-click a member world → the "Effective" line shows pin > group > global. Pin one world to see it win.
  6. Small-tile names appear as a tooltip on hover. Switch to Entities for portrait tiles.
